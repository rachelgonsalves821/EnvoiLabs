# ADR 0002. OpenInterviewer design review

Decision recorded for Gate 1 on 2026-11-21 under Rachel's delegated review. Applies to PRD 8.2 and 8.3, R-RSCH-08 and R-RSCH-09.

We reviewed OpenInterviewer at `8e020691798cfcbeddbf76dbf8e4a0a1f4e06d0d` under the MIT license. Envoi will adapt the following design patterns with its own code and data contracts. Upstream licensing and the files below were checked at the pinned revision. [License](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/blob/8e020691798cfcbeddbf76dbf8e4a0a1f4e06d0d/LICENSE)

| Pattern | Envoi decision | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Briefly quote the respondent's words when anchoring a neutral clarifying probe. The upstream interviewer varies depth by mode. | Keep the approved core question order. Retain Envoi's maximum of two probes per core question, the six-second p95 target and eight-second skip. A probe may use a brief excerpt from the current consented answer. | Gate 2 probe behavior tests cover approved order, non-leading wording, cap, timeout and advancing on a skipped probe. [Pinned interviewer prompt](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/blob/8e020691798cfcbeddbf76dbf8e4a0a1f4e06d0d/src/lib/prompts/interview.ts) |
| Persist the transcript before starting analysis. The upstream save path checks consent and revision and its worker claims analysis before reading. | Persist a scoped answer and durable job marker in one transaction. Recheck consent and transcript version before processing and redacted publication. Preserve idempotency when a job retries. | IT-1.5 covers repeated confirmation, dispatch retry, pending or failed redaction and withdrawn consent. [Pinned save route](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/blob/8e020691798cfcbeddbf76dbf8e4a0a1f4e06d0d/src/app/api/interviews/save/route.ts) · [Pinned worker](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/blob/8e020691798cfcbeddbf76dbf8e4a0a1f4e06d0d/src/lib/interviewAnalysis.ts) |
| Give the aggregate model a catalogue of previously located quotes. | At Gate 3, give the analysis model references to redacted excerpts with recorded transcript versions and exact offsets. Revalidate references before showing the report. An invalid or missing excerpt leaves the finding without that quote. | IT-3.2 covers quote matching, version drift, invalid offsets and study scope. [Pinned synthesis prompt](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/blob/8e020691798cfcbeddbf76dbf8e4a0a1f4e06d0d/src/lib/prompts/synthesis.ts) |
| Create a proposed follow-up study from synthesis with provenance checks. | Build an editable follow-up brief from the current verified report and unresolved hypotheses. Require researcher review of the brief and apply Envoi's feasibility, pricing and launch rules. | R-RSCH-09 checks that stale report evidence cannot populate the proposal and that the researcher approves the resulting study. [Pinned follow-up route](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/blob/8e020691798cfcbeddbf76dbf8e4a0a1f4e06d0d/src/app/api/studies/%5Bid%5D/generate-followup/route.ts) |

Envoi keeps its own study storage, invite routing, panel and payment policies, brand assets, and authoring screens. This review incorporates no third-party code or media. A later code adoption needs its own provenance, notice and license review and tests under [Build Plan section 11](../BUILD_PLAN.md#11-open-source-evaluation-and-reuse).
