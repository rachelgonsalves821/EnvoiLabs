# FindingCard

One theme in a study report, stated as a claim with the evidence that backs it.

The consumer provides the theme title (a full sentence claim), the count and total, a confidence level, the lead quote with its clip, respondent descriptor and quality score, and the link to all quotes.

- The title is the finding itself, written as a sentence a founder could repeat in a meeting.
- Counts are shown twice, as a dot strip (one dot per respondent, `ink` for "said this") and as text ("11 of 20 said this").
- Confidence is a status chip (`good` High, `warn` Medium, neutral Low) based on frequency and consistency.
- The lead quote sits in a `paper-sunk` well with a play button for the audio clip, shown only when the respondent opted in to share voice. Otherwise the button is absent and the text stands alone.
- Respondents are described by profile facts, never by name.

**Don't** show percentages under 30 respondents, use more than one quote per card, or put a finding on screen without a count.
